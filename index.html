Published Indian Patent

Breakthrough research bridging high-accuracy deep learning with extreme compute constraints on the industrial edge.

Indian Patent Publication App No.: 202641051630 A Date: 01 May 2026

Hybrid Knowledge Distillation and Quantization Framework for Lightweight Defect Classification

Industrial manufacturing environments require real-time surface defect classification on low-power edge nodes. This patent proposes a unified framework harmonizing teacher-student knowledge distillation with post-training INT8 quantization to dramatically shrink neural models while preserving state-of-the-art diagnostic accuracy.

Jurisdiction: Indian Patent Office (IPO)
Application Number: 202641051630 A
Publication Date: May 01, 2026
Inventors: Sarathy B & Research Team
Application Focus: Industrial Surface Defect Edge Classification
Framework Architecture
01

Teacher Representation

Heavyweight deep convolutional ensemble trained on extensive surface defect datasets.

02

Knowledge Distillation

Transfer of soft probabilities and representation manifolds to a lightweight student model.

03

Post-Training Quantization

FP32 to INT8 precision compression minimizing memory footprint by >70%.

04

Edge Acceleration

Deterministic sub-millisecond inference on low-power industrial sensor hardware.
