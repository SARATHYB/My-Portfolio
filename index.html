// About Me

My Story and my Journey

There was a time when everyone expected me to crack NEET, secure an MBBS seat, and become a doctor. But I chose a different path. I chose Engineering. I chose Data Science, even before AI began transforming the world.

For years, I questioned my decision. What if I failed? What if I had chosen the wrong path? But every time doubt knocked on my door, I reminded myself:

I WILL. · I SHOULD. · I MUST SUCCEED.

Today, I don't fear AI replacing jobs. I believe in using AI to empower humanity. Technology should not replace our purpose; it should help us fulfil it.

And if you think I am lucky, remember the journey behind the milestones, from writing my school public examinations during COVID-19 to earning a place at VIT Vellore, pursuing a degree I believe in, and stepping into the professional world through an internship amid an ever-evolving job market.

Beyond academics, my journey has taken me from volunteering at Riviera and Gravitas to taking on leadership and organiser-level responsibilities. Through NSS, I grew from helping with crowd control to becoming Publicity Head and organising special camps. I also contributed to more than three blood donation camps, coordinating with 8+ hospitals and helping mobilise over 1,000 student blood donors.

But the greatest rewards weren't just the titles or achievements. They were the people I met, the lives we touched, and the lessons that shaped me.

I haven't discovered my ultimate purpose yet, but I know I am here to build, lead, create, and contribute to something bigger than myself.

Becoming the Man, the Myth, the Legend that I always wanted to be!
And the best chapters are yet to come.

Published Indian Patent

Breakthrough research bridging high-accuracy deep learning with extreme compute constraints on the industrial edge.

Indian Patent Publication App No.: 202641051630 A Date: 01 May 2026

Hybrid Knowledge Distillation and Quantization Framework for Lightweight Defect Classification

Industrial manufacturing environments require real-time surface defect classification on low-power edge nodes. This patent proposes a unified framework harmonizing teacher-student knowledge distillation with post-training INT8 quantization to dramatically shrink neural models while preserving state-of-the-art diagnostic accuracy.

Jurisdiction: Indian Patent Office (IPO)
Application Number: 202641051630 A
Publication Date: May 01, 2026
Inventors: Sarathy B & Research Team
Application Focus: Industrial Surface Defect Edge Classification
Framework Architecture
01

Teacher Representation

Heavyweight deep convolutional ensemble trained on extensive surface defect datasets.

02

Knowledge Distillation

Transfer of soft probabilities and representation manifolds to a lightweight student model.

03

Post-Training Quantization

FP32 to INT8 precision compression minimizing memory footprint by >70%.

04

Edge Acceleration

Deterministic sub-millisecond inference on low-power industrial sensor hardware.
